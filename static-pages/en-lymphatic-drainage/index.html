Medical care

Therapeutic drainage

  • Cosmetic or reconstructive surgery

    Liposuction, tummy tuck, facelift, breast surgery… Manual drainage adapted to your tissues, combined if needed with taping, scar work and gentle rehabilitation, in coordination with your surgeon.

    Discover post-operative care

  • Lipedema

    A chronic disease of the fatty tissue, often mistaken for weight gain: disproportionate fat, mainly on the legs, tender to the touch, with a tendency to bruise.

    Manual drainage is a cornerstone of treatment, whether as conservative therapy or in preparation for liposuction. Diagnosed or suspected: let's talk about it at the assessment.

  • After cancer treatment

    After surgery or treatment for cancer, for example after lymph node removal, drainage helps prevent and manage lymphedema, in coordination with your medical team.

  • After varicose vein surgery

    To limit swelling and support your legs' recovery after the operation, following your vascular surgeon's instructions.

  • Heavy legs

    Heavy or swollen legs at the end of the day, venous insufficiency: the assessment helps take stock and adapt the treatment.

    Only occasional discomfort? Renata França drainage may also suit you.

Is drainage reimbursed?

Very rarely, even with a prescription. The French national health insurance (Assurance Maladie) only covers it in a few cases:

  • Primary lymphedema
  • Cancer care
  • Chronic venous conditions

This is determined by the angiologist's written diagnosis and my assessment. In these cases, treatment takes place in Lyon 5. In all other cases, sessions are charged at the prices below.

Therapeutic or wellness: the difference

After an operation, drainage must be adapted to the condition of your tissues and to your surgeon's instructions: it is not a wellness session.
